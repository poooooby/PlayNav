NaviPlay - credits for the files in assets/ and for adapted code
===============================================================

Super Training stadium (assets/stadiumlarge.png)
------------------------------------------------
The backdrop behind the Balloon Bots during a Super-Training Regimen: Wooble's own recreation
of the scene, made for NaviPlay (1920x1920, so it covers the whole top screen).

Pokemon-Amie cottage (assets/amie_cottage.png)
----------------------------------------------
The top screen while you decorate the Amie room: Wooble's own art, made for NaviPlay
(1920x1920, so it covers the whole top screen).

Pokemon-Amie play sky (assets/amie-play-bg.png)
-----------------------------------------------
The top screen while you play with your Pokemon: a deep blue dotted sky with the rainbow in the
lower right, Wooble's own art, made for NaviPlay (1920x1920, so it covers the whole top screen).

Hover board (assets/hoverboard.png)
-----------------------------------
The board your Pokemon rides during a Super-Training Regimen: Wooble's own pixel art, made for
NaviPlay.

Super Training regimen picker music (assets/stmenu.ogg)
-------------------------------------------------------
The music while you choose a Super-Training Regimen, when the card image's own track has not
been extracted: Wooble's original music, made for NaviPlay.

Sprite and music (assets/r-deal.png, assets/UnseenEncounter.ogg)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A 64x64 sprite and a music track ("Unseen Encounter"): Wooble's own pixel art and original
music, made for NaviPlay.

Balloon Bot sprites (assets/balloon_bots.png)
---------------------------------------------
The Pokemon every Super-Training Balloon Bot is made in the image of (Mega Aggron too): the
battler front sprites of the Gen 9 Pack for Pokemon Essentials, halved to their 96x96 art and
packed into one atlas of colour numbers with the palettes in data/balloon_bots.lua (by
tools/balloon_atlas.py). From the pack's credits, "Pokemon Battler Sprites":

  Gen 1-5 Pokemon Sprites  - veekun
  Gen 6 Pokemon Sprites    - All Contributors To Smogon X/Y Sprite Project

The Gen 6 bots are Noibat, Noivern, Dragalge, Tyrantrum, Aurorus, Aegislash, Scatterbug,
Spewpa, Vivillon and Mega Aggron; the rest are Gen 1-5. Pokemon and their designs are
(c) Nintendo / Creatures Inc. / GAME FREAK Inc.; NaviPlay is an unofficial fan project.

Tile Puzzle trees (assets/amie_tile.png)
----------------------------------------
The trees on Tile Puzzle's shores (a spruce, an oak, a row of pines and a bush, by day and by
night), from TREE PIXEL PACK (128) by AstroCom (https://astrocom.space), shrunk into the
scene's sheet and packed into one atlas of 16-bit colour numbers with the palettes in
data/amie_tile.lua (by tools/tile_trees.py). The rest of Tile Puzzle's picture - its sky, sea, night stars and photo
frames - is drawn by NaviPlay (src/amie/ui_art.lua).

PLAY treeline (assets/amie_scene.png)
-------------------------------------
The band of trees along PLAY's horizon, built from PIXEL BUSHES PACK by AstroCom
(https://astrocom.space): its round bushes, shrubs and berry bushes shrunk, turned to the
scene's teal and arranged into the strip, packed into one atlas of 16-bit colour numbers with the
palette in data/amie_scene.lua (by tools/play_bushes.py).

PLAY lawn (assets/amie_grass.png)
---------------------------------
PLAY's lawn and the grass standing on it, from the GRASS+ tile sheet by SciGho: a ground tile
turned to the lawn's bright green under the scene's lighter oval, and the sheet's grass tufts,
clump and blades turned to the lawn's greens, packed into one atlas of 16-bit colour numbers
with the palettes in data/amie_grass.lua (by tools/play_grass.py).

Split-screen layout (src/layout.lua Layout.gear, Layout.fitRect)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
How the split screen shares the window between the game and the touch screen - the shares,
the gap, the bottom safe area, the swap and the fit - is adapted from Kanto Gear's
THEME:windowLayout and THEME:fitRect, by AverageConsumer, used with the author's permission:
https://github.com/AverageConsumer/kanto-gear
