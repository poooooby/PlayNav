Item icons (assets/items/)
--------------------------
The icons of the items Super Training hands out that Gen 3 never had, copied from
PokeSprite by Michiel Sikma (msikma) and contributors:
https://github.com/msikma/pokesprite

  health-wing.png   Health Wing
  muscle-wing.png   Muscle Wing
  resist-wing.png   Resist Wing
  genius-wing.png   Genius Wing
  clever-wing.png   Clever Wing
  swift-wing.png    Swift Wing
  pretty-wing.png   Pretty Wing
  dusk-stone.png    Dusk Stone
  shiny-stone.png   Shiny Stone
  dawn-stone.png    Dawn Stone
